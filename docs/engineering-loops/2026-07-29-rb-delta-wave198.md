# RB catalogue wave 198 — DELTA official-source enrichment

Date: 2026-07-29

Market: `microchips-by` (`ru-BY`)

Scope: existing Bitrix industrial-battery cards only. The wave does not create new products and does not authorize prices, stock claims, images or indexability.

## Result

- The raw DELTA slice contains 134 records. Four ambiguous repeated base models were stopped before evidence matching, leaving 130 candidates.
- The current official DELTA catalogue exposed exact product pages for 50 candidates. Eight of them collided by URL with existing 1C identities and two more collided by direct MPN, so all ten are pinned in an explicit identity-hold registry.
- Forty non-conflicting cards received exact manufacturer, MPN, VRLA AGM technology, voltage and capacity facts from their official product pages.
- The remaining 80 candidates stay unchanged until an exact official page or datasheet is acquired. No model-family inference is treated as product evidence.
- All 40 updated cards remain `availability=on_request`, `price=null` and `noindex`; no `Offer` schema was emitted.

## Evidence and safeguards

- Official series/catalog pages: `dt`, `dtm`, `dtm_l`, `ft_m`, `fts_x`, `hr`, `hrl_x`, `hrl_w`, `stc` and the `cgd` catalogue filter on `https://delta-batt.com/`.
- 125 current official product pages were fetched; 50 exact candidate pages were retained as immutable HTML snapshots with their SHA-256 hashes.
- Candidate identity state is pinned to `catalog_identity_candidates@2026-07-29T03:24:05.314496Z;source-run=763;rows=0` and snapshot SHA-256 `9a7b9a19c889b759f311a180b0a6bc7286c90f250dac4490b248b91564449eb2`.
- Decimal punctuation remains significant, preventing the false identity collision `HR 12-4.5` ↔ `HR 12-45`.
- Product evidence is accepted only when the official page path is under `/products/`, the H1 exactly matches the candidate model and the downloaded file hash matches the registry.
- Extraction is restricted to the product-information block before recommendations; voltage and capacity from related products cannot leak into the card.
- Existing identities are checked twice: before content staging and again before preview URL materialization. This second gate found ten holds that the initial source slice could not prove.
