# Duplicate service rows excluded from the RB product catalogue

`КА-00003360` and `КА-00003363` are two non-published RB drafts with the
identical name **«Тестирование аккумуляторов в батарее»**. Both have no
manufacturer, SKU, MPN or technical attributes. They are a service activity,
not separately purchasable catalogue goods; leaving them as two product pages
would create duplicate, thin SEO inventory.

The exclusion removes only the two RB `site_products` links. It does not delete
the canonical 1C records, nor does it prevent documenting the service later as
one verified service page with a real scope and terms.
