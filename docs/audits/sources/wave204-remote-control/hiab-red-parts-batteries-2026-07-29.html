Hiab Red Parts

The Hiab Red Parts line is a growing portfolio of cost-effective parts for the maintenance and repair of equipment that is nearing the end of its operational lifespan. Red Parts ensure that older Hiab models can continue to operate efficiently and safely with the lowest possible cost.

Hiab Red Parts offer an affordable and reliable alternative to more costly Hiab Original Parts when your focus is on reducing the overall ownership and operation costs of ageing equipment fleets. All Red Parts are covered by a 12-month warranty for extra peace of mind.

Why choose Red Parts?

  • Peace of mind – guaranteed compatibility plus a 12-month warranty
  • Lower total cost of ownership – Hiab-approved parts at a lower cost
  • Minimal equipment downtime – high availability for a wide range of parts
